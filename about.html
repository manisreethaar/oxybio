About

A food biotechnology company at laboratory stage.

Oxygen Bioinnovations Private Limited was incorporated in 2026 to build everyday food on indigenous Indian grains, with the evidence in place before the claims.

Our story

Built from frustration.

The way India eats has changed faster in one generation than in the centuries before it. Much of what fills the gap is over-processed and sold on what is printed on the pack. The supply chain is built for shelf life and margin; whether the food does anything for the person eating it is rarely measured.

We started from a simpler question: what would it take to build an everyday food whose value is measured, not asserted, and which is built on grains that have always been part of the Indian diet?

Answering it properly meant owning the research environment. The contract manufacturers we spoke to advised simplifying the formula and using cheaper carriers. A technology business incubator gave us a laboratory, mentorship and the time to do the work slowly and in the open. That is why we are at TBI-DETI@ACE in Hosur.

Vision and mission

Vision

A world where traditional Indian food knowledge and modern nutrition science point to the same answer.

Mission

To build everyday foods that are honestly formulated, grounded in evidence, and priced for daily life.

Principles

Three commitments.

Evidence before claims.

Every statement has a source, and nothing is claimed before it is measured. If the evidence is weak, we say so. If we are wrong, we correct it publicly.

Respect for traditional food knowledge.

Indigenous grains are Indian food culture, not exotic superfoods. We cite that knowledge correctly and build on it honestly.

Negative results reported.

If something does not work as expected, the updates log will say so. The process matters as much as the product.

Roadmap

Where we are.

Each stage gates the next. Nothing is sold before the last one.

  1. Complete

    Problem analysis

    Review of national nutrition datasets, including NFHS-5, ICMR and WHO India sources, to define the gap the work addresses.

  2. Complete

    Product concept

    A fermented base made from indigenous Indian grains, developed in two formats.

  3. Current

    Phase 0: laboratory validation

    Development and characterisation of the base at TBI-DETI@ACE, Hosur.

  4. Next

    Prototype development

    Laboratory-scale batches of each format, with several iterations expected.

  5. Later

    Testing and licensing

    Sensory evaluation, shelf-life studies, accredited third-party safety testing and the FSSAI licence application.

  6. Later

    Pilot production and launch

    Only after an FSSAI licence is granted.
