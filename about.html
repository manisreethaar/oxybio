CHAPTER 01: THE ORIGIN

The Hook

I always had a dream of starting a business on my own. I was fed up with working for someone else's growth. Why work 8 to 10 hours a day for someone else, instead of working those same hours for your own growth to make something useful? We all have one life—we should do something remarkable.

Why this specific problem? Due to modernization, our lifestyles have changed dramatically. The core issue behind most lifestyle problems revolves around the food we are eating—processed, low quality, chemical-filled, and sold on fake promises. There is an ancient Tamil quote, "Unnave marundhu" (Food is medicine), but we have forgotten it.

Radical Transparency //

My idea is to provide healthy, quality, scientifically-backed food without fake promises. With my background in biotechnology, I want to use my knowledge to actually solve this. Unvarnished truth? I don't know a lot of things yet. I don't know if this precise idea will work, if I can make the prototype exactly right, or if it will behave the way I expect. A lot of things can go wrong.

"But I am building this for everyone who deserves real, scientifically-backed nutrition—starting with the professionals, students, and athletes who need it most."

Chapter 04

Our Approach

Six absolute clinical methodologies governing every extraction, formulation, and biological model produced in this laboratory.

01

Methodological Precedence

Formula specifications are locked before any visual or presentation mapping occurs. Biochemical targets dictate the form factor. The biological mechanism is the singular constraint.
02

Genomic Specificity

We do not import exogenous baselines. All active compounds and concentrations are calibrated strictly against endogenous physiological deficits identified within Indian genetic sub-populations.
03

Open-Source Validation

Certificates of Analysis (CoA) and liquid chromatography mass spectrometry (LC-MS) data arrays will be public by default. Efficacy data is classified strictly into 'preliminary hypothesis' or 'robust literature'.
04

Pharmacological Thresholds

All extractions are formulated exclusively at or above the Minimum Effective Dose (MED) established in double-blind, placebo-controlled literature. Sub-threshold inclusions for labeling purposes are prohibited.
05

Absorption Kinetics

Absolute mass is irrelevant to clinical outcome. 100mg at 5% bioavailability yields 5mg intracellularly. We engineer solely for maximal systemic circulation and cellular uptake velocity, not mass density.
06

Pre-Registered Validation

Commercialization and scaling operations are strictly gated behind the final conclusion of registered clinical efficacy trials evaluating the exact multi-compound physiological response.
