CHAPTER 01

The Hook

We spent six months trying to find a daily nutrition product we would actually recommend to someone we cared about. We could not find one. Either the science was inadequate, or the ingredients were compromised, or the price was inaccessible, or it was designed for a Western diet and simply repacked for India.

Diagnostic Result //

We analyzed extensive clinical data from ICMR, NFHS-5, and WHO India. The patterns across urban demographics were consistent and alarming: the vast majority of health-conscious individuals exhibit at least one critical nutritional deficiency without realizing it.

The food system was failing people who were trying to do the right thing.
"We decided the only option was to build it ourselves."

CHAPTER 02

The Journey So Far

What we have built before we built anything physical. Most companies build first and think about science second. We spent months thinking about the science before building anything.

COMPLETED

Systematically analyzed ICMR, NFHS-5, and WHO India datasets. Mapped exact deficiency profiles across urban demographic segments. Built the scientific evidence base that defines the problem our products solve.

Designed complete evidence-based formulations for 3 functional beverages and 1 nutrition bar. Every ingredient selected from peer-reviewed literature. Every dose set at clinically relevant thresholds — not label decoration doses.

WHERE WE ARE NOW

DETI@ACE - TBI, Hosur

Accepted by the DETI@ACE - TBI at Adhiyamaan College of Engineering, Hosur. Currently establishing lab access, sourcing ingredient suppliers, and laying the operational groundwork for prototype development.

UP NEXT

Prototype Development

Lab-scale formulation batches will be developed at the TBI facility. This is where the theoretical formulations meet real ingredients, real equipment, and real taste. Multiple iterations expected per product.

FUTURE

Testing, Validation & Clinical Study

Sensory evaluation, shelf-life studies, FSSAI compliance testing, and a planned 135-participant, 8-week clinical validation study — all completed before any product goes to market. Results published regardless of outcome.

FUTURE

Manufacturing scale-up, regulated commercial production, and market launch. Distribution built around transparency — with every formulation decision, every test result, and every ingredient source available for public review.

Chapter 04

Our Approach

Six principles that separate evidence-based nutrition from everything else on the market.

01

Science Before Marketing

We designed the formulation before we designed the brand. We chose ingredients before we chose colors. This is backwards from how most nutrition companies work. We think it is the only sensible order.

02

India Is Not a Market Segment

We did not take a Western formula and add Ashwagandha to make it Indian. We started from India. From what Indian bodies are deficient in. From what Indian ingredients can provide.

03

Transparency Is Not Optional

We publish our lab reports. We name our ingredient suppliers. We cite the studies behind our claims. We tell you when a study is preliminary and when it is robust.

04

Dose Matters

Ashwagandha at 50mg is not the same as Ashwagandha at 300mg. We formulate at clinically relevant doses — not at doses that merely allow us to list the ingredient on the label.

05

Bioavailability First

A 100mg dose with 5% absorption delivers 5mg to your body. A 50mg dose with 35% absorption delivers 17.5mg. The nutrient that reaches your bloodstream is the only one that matters.

06

Clinical Evidence

We committed to designing our clinical validation study before manufacturing any product — not because regulations require it, but because no company should sell a health product without a pre-committed plan to prove it works.
