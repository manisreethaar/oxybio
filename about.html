Origin Protocol

Built from
frustration.

Not a corporate origin story. The honest account of a problem we couldn't ignore.

I always had a dream of starting a business, but more fundamentally, I was fed up with working for someone else's growth. Why work 8 or 10 hours a day for someone else's goals, when you can use the exact same hours to build something for yourself? We all have one life: I wanted to do something remarkable and actually useful.

Due to technology development and modernization, our lifestyle has changed dramatically. We are encountering sweeping lifestyle problems, and for most of these issues, the core driver revolves around the food we are eating — processed, low quality, filled with chemicals, and packaged with fake promises. There is an ancient Tamil quote, "Unnave marundhu" (food is medicine), but we have completely forgotten it as a society. This amnesia is causing massive health and environmental impacts globally.

My idea was simple: provide healthy, nutritional, scientifically-backed food without fake promises. With my background in biotechnology, I wanted to work in a domain where I actually have knowledge. But the unvarnished truth? I don't know a lot of things. I don't know if this precise idea will work, if I can make the prototype exactly right, or if it will behave the way I expect. A lot of things can go wrong.

The Solution Engineering //

I am trying to build a solution using fundamentally powerful substrates like mushrooms and millets — engineering them through advanced biotechnological concepts like probiotics, targeted fermentation, and nano-encapsulation to unlock their innate potential.

"I am building this for everyone who deserves good, healthy, scientifically-backed real food — concentrating right now on working professionals, students, and athletes who face acute issues like skipping breakfast, lacking core nutrition, memory strain, and recovery barriers."

The Non-Negotiables
OUR 03 PRINCIPLES
01
Evidence Mandate

We will never publish a claim we cannot prove.

Every ingredient decision has a source. Every source is linked. If the evidence is weak, we say so. If we are wrong, we correct it publicly.

NO FAKE PROMISES
02
Respect The Roots

We will respect traditional Indian food knowledge.

Ragi, Moringa, Kokum, Ashwagandha — these are not exotic superfoods. They are Indian food culture. We do not appropriate them. We understand them, cite them correctly, and build on them honestly.

ANTI-APPROPRIATION
03
Transparent Process

We will publish negative results, not just positive ones.

If a formulation does not achieve what we expected, we will say so. The build log exists because the process of getting it right is as important as the product at the end.

THE BUILD LOG

"These are not aspirational statements written for a pitch deck. They are the conditions under which this company was built — and the standard we will be held to publicly. If we ever violate any of these, it will be visible on our website, because we will have written the correction ourselves."

Direction

Where we are going.
And why it matters.

V
01 / Vision

A world where traditional Indian food wisdom and modern nutritional science are not in conflict.

A world where what your grandmother knew and what a clinical trial proves point to the same answer. We did not invent this idea. Thirumoolar wrote it 1,500 years ago. We are just making it true again — making eating well a daily habit, not a privilege.

M
02 / Mission

To build functional foods that are honestly formulated, evidence-grounded, and accessible.

We build for the urban professional who skips breakfast, the student running on coffee, and the athlete who can't afford imported supplements. Not premium wellness products for a narrow market. Not marketing dressed up as science. Just food that earns its claims.
