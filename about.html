Why we started

Labels were doing the work that food should do.

Health drinks in India are sold on what is printed on the pack. We started from a different question: what would it take to build a daily food whose value is measured, not asserted, and which is built on grains that have always been part of the Indian diet?

There is a Tamil phrase, உணவே மருந்து (unave marundhu), that is usually translated as "food is medicine". We take it as a reason to do the science properly, not as a claim.

Principles

Three commitments.

We will not publish a claim we cannot support.

Every statement has a source. If the evidence is weak, we say so. If we are wrong, we correct it publicly.

We respect traditional Indian food knowledge.

Indigenous grains are Indian food culture, not exotic superfoods. We cite that knowledge correctly and build on it honestly.

We report negative results.

If something does not work as expected, the updates log will say so. The process matters as much as the product.

Roadmap

Where we are.

Each stage gates the next. Nothing is sold before the last one.

  1. Complete

    Problem analysis

    Review of national nutrition datasets, including NFHS-5, ICMR and WHO India sources, to define the gap the work addresses.

  2. Complete

    Product concept

    A fermented indigenous grain base, reconstituted in milk or carbonated water.

  3. Current, since April 2026

    Phase 0: laboratory validation

    Development and characterisation of the grain base at TBI-DETI@ACE, Hosur.

  4. Next

    Prototype development

    Laboratory-scale batches of each format, with multiple iterations expected.

  5. Later

    Testing and licensing

    Sensory evaluation, shelf-life studies, accredited third-party safety testing and the FSSAI licence application.

  6. Later

    Pilot production and launch

    Only after an FSSAI licence is granted.
