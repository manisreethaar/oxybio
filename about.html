Origin Protocol

Built from
frustration.

Not a corporate origin story. The honest account of a problem we couldn't ignore.

Our lifestyle has changed dramatically, but our biological algorithms haven't. We are encountering sweeping metabolic and lifestyle problems, and the core driver is the food we are eatingover-processed, chemically preserved, and packaged with fake promises. The modern food infrastructure optimizes for shelf-life and margins, not bioavailability or health. We got tired of watching consumers pay premium prices for unverified promises.

01 // THE BIOLOGICAL RESPONSE

There is an ancient Tamil quote, "Unnave Marundhu" (food is medicine), but we have completely forgotten it as a society. Our objective isn't to invent new synthetic superfoods; it's to apply modern biotechnological rigor to restore the efficacy of indigenous ingredients. We are engineering fundamentally powerful substrates like mushrooms and indigenous grains through controlled lactic acid fermentation and functional extraction to unlock their innate potential.

With a background in biotechnology, the path was clearbut the execution is complex. The unvarnished truth? We don't have all the answers yet. We don't know if every formulation will behave exactly the way we expect at scale. That is exactly why we maintain an open ledger of our clinical validations.

"I am building this for everyone who deserves scientifically-backed, real foodconcentrating right now on working professionals, students, and athletes who face acute issues like skipping breakfast, lacking core nutrition, and recovery barriers."

The Non-Negotiables
OUR 03 PRINCIPLES
Evidence Mandate

We will never publish a claim we cannot prove.

Every ingredient decision has a source. Every source is linked. If the evidence is weak, we say so. If we are wrong, we correct it publicly.

NO FAKE PROMISES
Respect The Roots

We will respect traditional Indian food knowledge.

Indigenous Indian grains, legumes, and botanicals we study for future-pipeline exploration are not exotic superfoods. They are Indian food culture. We do not appropriate them. We understand them, cite them correctly, and build on them honestly.

ANTI-APPROPRIATION
Transparent Process

We will publish negative results, not just positive ones.

If a formulation does not achieve what we expected, we will say so. The build log exists because the process of getting it right is as important as the product at the end.

Direction

Where we are going.
And why it matters.

V
01 / Vision

A world where traditional Indian food wisdom and modern nutritional science are not in conflict.

A world where what your grandmother knew and what a clinical trial proves point to the same answer. We did not invent this idea. Thirumoolar wrote it 1,500 years ago. We are just making it true again. making eating well a daily habit, not a privilege.

M
02 / Mission

To build functional foods that are honestly formulated, evidence-grounded, and accessible.

We build for the urban professional who skips breakfast, the student running on coffee, and the athlete who can't afford imported supplements. Not premium wellness products for a narrow market. Not marketing dressed up as science. Just food that earns its claims.

What Makes OBI Difficult to Replicate

Structural Advantages.

Advantages that compound over time. not just a product, but a platform built on process discipline, supply chain moats, and digital infrastructure.

Advantage 01

Pharma-Grade Fermentation Pedigree

Our CEO’s prior role in pharma-grade microbial upstream R&D gives us process rigour that consumer-food competitors typically lack. We apply pharmaceutical process discipline to food fermentation. GMP thinking from day one.

Advantage 02

Species-Specific Extraction Protocols

The Indian industry applies a generic extraction protocol to all mushrooms. We have developed separate, proprietary protocols for each species. optimised to protect distinct bioactive compounds that degrade under standard conditions. This is a technical moat.

Advantage 03

OxyOS. Digital GMP Platform

A fully functional digital batch management platform. built before revenue. SOP compliance tracking, CAPA, inventory, and full batch traceability from raw material lot to final product. Infrastructure most food startups build after Series A.

Advantage 04

Platform Economics & Extensibility

One fermentation architecture → unlimited grain + mushroom combinations. New SKUs without process re-engineering. Indigenous grain sourcing and domestic mushroom cultivation partnerships are supply chain moats. Aligned with Make in India · National Millet Mission · Atmanirbhar Bharat.

Go-to-Market

Channel Strategy.

A phased channel approach starting with high-trust, low-cost D2C channels and building toward full national distribution. All commercial activity is gated on FSSAI State Manufacturing Licence grant.

01
D2C. Owned Channels

WhatsApp-first community + owned website. College campus and professional network seeding. Zero platform dependency at launch.

02
Quick Commerce

Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart. Bengaluru and Hosur priority. No cold chain required. Ambient shelf-stable product is a logistics fit.

03
Institutional B2B

College canteens, gym front-desks, co-working spaces. Recurring bulk orders with brand visibility. Beachhead: Adhiyamaan College of Engineering (5,000 students).

04
Online Marketplace

Amazon & Flipkart in Phase 2 once brand credibility and reviews are established through D2C and institutional channels.
