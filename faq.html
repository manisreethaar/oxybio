FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Short answers, with links to the page that has the detail.

The company

What is Oxygen Bioinnovations?

A food biotechnology research and development startup building everyday food on Indian grains. More about us.

Are you a product company or a research company?

Both, by design. We are at research and development stage today, and what we research is intended to reach people as products once it has been validated and licensed.

Are you a registered company?

Yes. Oxygen Bioinnovations Private Limited is incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, and is a DPIIT Recognised Startup. Registration numbers are on the home page.

Can I visit?

Visits are by appointment only. Write to us with the purpose of your visit.

The product

What are you making?

An everyday food built on Indian grains. The platform page explains what we are building.

Can I buy it?

Not yet. Join the waitlist to hear when there is news.

Do your products have proven health benefits?

No product claims have been made or evaluated. What we are investigating is listed on the platform page, and results will be reported on the updates page.

Working here

Where can I find open roles, internships, pay and leave?

Everything about working here is on the careers page.

What is the culture like?

Curious, rigorous and honest. Everyone is expected to question and contribute; if an experiment is designed badly, say so. Data integrity is not negotiable.

Investment and collaboration

Are you open to investment?

Yes, particularly from investors who understand research-led timelines. See the investor section.

Can we collaborate on research?

Yes, with universities, research institutions and complementary companies, under a formal agreement. Start a conversation.

Do you offer contract research?

Yes, in functional food formulation and food process development. Tell us what you need.

Still have a question? Write to us. You will get a reply from a person.
