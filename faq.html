FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

About the company, the product, working here, and investing or collaborating with us.

The company

What is Oxygen Bioinnovations?

A food biotechnology research and development startup based at TBI-DETI@ACE, Adhiyamaan College of Engineering, Hosur, Tamil Nadu. We are developing a fermented base made from indigenous Indian grains for everyday drinks.

Are you a product company or a research company?

Both, by design. We are at research and development stage today, and everything we research is intended to reach people as real products once it has been validated and licensed.

Are you a registered company?

Yes. Oxygen Bioinnovations Private Limited is incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U72100TZ2026PTC038160) and is a DPIIT Recognised Startup (DIPP258270). We are incubated at TBI-DETI@ACE, Adhiyamaan College of Engineering, Hosur.

Where are you located? Can I visit?

We operate from TBI-DETI@ACE on the Adhiyamaan College of Engineering campus in Hosur. Visits are by appointment only: write to info@oxygenbioinnovations.com with the purpose of your visit.

The product

What are you making?

A fermented powder made from indigenous Indian grains, designed to be reconstituted in milk or in carbonated water. Both formats are in development. See the platform page.

Can I buy it?

No. Nothing is for sale. We will not sell any product until an FSSAI licence has been granted; the application is to be filed. You can join the waitlist to hear when there is news.

Do your products have proven health benefits?

No product claims have been made or evaluated. What we are investigating is listed on the platform page, and results will be reported on the updates page as they are measured.

Working here

What does a Research Associate actually do?

Designs and runs laboratory experiments, develops and tests formulations, analyses data, keeps research documentation, reads the literature, and contributes directly to product decisions. There is no layer of bureaucracy between the bench and the business.

What qualifications do you look for?

A background in Biotechnology, Microbiology, Food Science, Biochemistry or a related life science. Beyond that: curiosity, the ability to work independently, comfort with ambiguity, willingness to learn across disciplines, and integrity in your work.

Is my job secure at a startup?

An early-stage startup carries more uncertainty than an established company, and we will not pretend otherwise. We are building with long-term intent, are backed by a technology business incubator, and are actively pursuing grants and investment.

How do I apply?

Send your CV and a short note (three to five sentences) on why you want to work here specifically to careers@oxygenbioinnovations.com. Applications that show no awareness of our work are not shortlisted.

Internships

Who can apply for an internship?

Undergraduate and postgraduate students in Biotechnology, Microbiology, Food Science, Biochemistry or related fields, from any Indian college or university. Internships run from one to six months.

Are internships paid?

Paid internships are offered to students contributing to active research for three to six months; the stipend is discussed at selection. Shorter or academic-project internships are unpaid and include a completion certificate, a work experience letter and a recommendation letter. We never charge a fee for an internship.

What will I learn?

Hands-on laboratory work from the first week: experiment design, formulation development, stability testing, documentation and literature review, with direct, hands-on mentorship.

Pay and culture

What is the salary for research positions?

Competitive for an incubator-stage startup, not comparable to a large established company. Specific pay is discussed individually; the most recent Research Associate role was advertised at ₹18,000 to ₹25,000 per month. We also offer direct mentorship and the chance to co-author research outputs.

When is salary paid?

On or before the fifth working day of the following month, by bank transfer, with a monthly pay slip. Intern stipends follow the same schedule.

What is the culture like?

Curious, rigorous and honest. Everyone is expected to question and contribute; if an experiment is designed badly, say so. Data integrity is not negotiable. Startups are demanding, but we will never create stress through poor management or unclear expectations.

What is your policy on workplace harassment?

Zero tolerance. We comply with the POSH Act, have clear grievance procedures, and handle every complaint confidentially. Leave and working hours are set out on the careers page.

Investment and collaboration

Are you open to investment?

Yes. We are seeking non-dilutive grant funding and strategic seed investment, particularly from investors who understand deep-tech and research-led timelines. See the investor section or get in touch.

Can we collaborate on research?

Yes, with universities, research institutions and complementary companies. All collaborations are governed by a formal MOU or collaboration agreement. Start a conversation.

Do you offer contract research?

Yes, in functional food formulation and food process development. Write to info@oxygenbioinnovations.com with your requirements.

Still have a question? Write to us. You will get a reply from a person.
