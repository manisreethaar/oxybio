FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Short answers, with links to the page that has the detail.

The company

What is Oxygen Bioinnovations?

A food biotechnology research and development startup in Hosur, Tamil Nadu, building everyday food on Indian grains. More about us.

Are you a product company or a research company?

Both, by design. We are at research and development stage today, and what we research is intended to reach people as products once it has been validated and licensed.

Are you a registered company?

Yes. Oxygen Bioinnovations Private Limited is incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, and is a DPIIT Recognised Startup. Registration details are on the about page.

Can I visit?

Visits are by appointment only. Write to us with the purpose of your visit.

The product

What are you making?

One grain base, in development in two formats. Details are on the platform page.

Can I buy it?

No. Nothing will be sold until an FSSAI licence has been granted, and the application is to be filed. Join the waitlist to hear when there is news.

Do your products have proven health benefits?

No product claims have been made or evaluated. What we are investigating is listed on the platform page, and results will be reported on the updates page.

Working here

What does a Research Associate do?

Designs and runs laboratory experiments, develops and tests formulations, analyses data, keeps research documentation and contributes directly to product decisions. Details of current roles are on the careers page.

What qualifications do you look for?

A background in Biotechnology, Microbiology, Food Science, Biochemistry or a related life science, plus curiosity, independence, comfort with ambiguity and integrity in your work.

Is my job secure at a startup?

An early-stage startup carries more uncertainty than an established company, and we will not pretend otherwise. We are building with long-term intent, backed by a technology business incubator, and actively pursuing grants and investment.

How do I apply?

By email, as described on the careers page.

Internships

Who can apply, and are internships paid?

Undergraduate and postgraduate students in related fields, for one to six months. Longer research internships are paid. Eligibility and terms are on the careers page. We never charge a fee for an internship.

What will I learn?

Hands-on laboratory work from the first week: experiment design, formulation development, stability testing, documentation and literature review, with direct, hands-on mentorship.

Pay and culture

What are pay and leave like?

Pay is competitive for an incubator-stage startup and paid by the fifth working day of the following month. The full leave policy, including cycle leave, is on the careers page.

What is the culture like?

Curious, rigorous and honest. Everyone is expected to question and contribute; if an experiment is designed badly, say so. Data integrity is not negotiable.

What is your policy on workplace harassment?

Zero tolerance. We comply with the POSH Act, have clear grievance procedures, and handle every complaint confidentially.

Investment and collaboration

Are you open to investment?

Yes, particularly from investors who understand research-led timelines. See the investor section.

Can we collaborate on research?

Yes, with universities, research institutions and complementary companies, under a formal agreement. Start a conversation.

Do you offer contract research?

Yes, in functional food formulation and food process development. Tell us what you need.

Still have a question? Write to us. You will get a reply from a person.
