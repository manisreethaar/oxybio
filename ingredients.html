BIOAVAILABLE CALCIUM

Finger Millet (Ragi)

Testing sprouting and micro-milling protocols to target a 60% reduction in phytic acid, aiming to unlock bioavailable calcium without anti-nutrient interference.

STANDARDIZATION 344mg Ca / 100g
CLINICAL REFERENCE DOSE 8,000mg
NATURAL IRON

Pearl Millet (Bajra)

Targeting natural iron retention and slowly digestible starch profiles. Our current hypothesis relies on preserving its structure to prevent insulin spikes.

STANDARDIZATION 8mg Fe / 100g
CLINICAL REFERENCE DOSE 4,000mg

NEUROGENESIS (NGF)

Lion's Mane Extract

Currently testing hot-water extraction of the fruiting body (fruiting body hot-water protocol). The target is isolating hericenones and erinacines for Nerve Growth Factor stimulation.

STANDARDIZATION ≥30% β-Glucans
CLINICAL REFERENCE DOSE 1,000mg
CELLULAR ATP

Cordyceps Militaris

Investigating cordycepin profiles for cellular oxygen utilization and ATP production. The target is delaying fatigue at the mitochondrial level.

STANDARDIZATION ≥1% Cordycepin
CLINICAL REFERENCE DOSE 500mg
IMMUNE MODULATION

Reishi (Ganoderma)

Investigating beta-glucan standardization for immunomodulatory activity. Published literature shows Ganoderma beta-glucans interact with macrophage and NK-cell pathways.

STANDARDIZATION ≥30% Beta-Glucan
CLINICAL REFERENCE DOSE 400mg
