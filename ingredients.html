EXP_02 Co-factors

NEUROLOGICAL BASELINE

Methylcobalamin (B12)

The active, bioavailable form of Vitamin B12. Plant-predominant diets have zero B12 — fermented millet cannot provide it either. 65% of Indian vegetarians are deficient. Targeting methylcobalamin (not cyanocobalamin) for direct cellular utilization without conversion.

FORM Methylcobalamin (active)
TARGET DOSE 500mcg
CALCIUM UTILIZATION

Vitamin D3 + K2 (MK-7)

Ragi provides 344mg Ca/100g — but calcium without D3 absorbs at under 20%. D3 (cholecalciferol) enables intestinal calcium absorption; K2-MK7 directs it to bone matrix, not arterial walls. 70–90% of urban Indians are Vitamin D deficient. These two co-factors complete what Ragi starts.

D3 DOSE 1,000 IU Cholecalciferol
K2 DOSE 45mcg MK-7

Adaptogen Protocol

EFFICACY TARGET

Ashwagandha KSM-66®

Sourcing the most heavily researched KSM-66 root extract. Our formulation models rely on replicating its double-blind clinical trial results for cortisol reduction and stress markers.

EXTRACTION Milk/Water (No Alcohol)
WITHANOLIDES ≥5%
CONCENTRATION 600mg

Why not standard Ashwagandha?

Our research avoids generic leaf extracts due to cytotoxic Withaferin A levels. We are validating 100% root extraction protocols for safety and maximal functional efficacy.

  • Targeting robust perceived stress reduction
  • Targeting measurable serum cortisol reduction
  • Investigating VO2 max improvements
