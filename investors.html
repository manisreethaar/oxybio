02 — Market Opportunity

A $6.9B market
with zero Indian RTD brands
combining both technologies.

India's functional beverages market was valued at $6.9B (₹57,000 Cr) in 2025 and is projected to grow at 10.74% CAGR through 2034, reaching $18.8B (Grand View Research, 2024).

No Indian RTD beverage brand currently combines LAB fermentation with medicinal mushroom bioactives. Emerging Indian fermented millet RTDs (BlissBody, milSipp) are gut-health only — no mushroom bioactives.

The Gap — What No Indian RTD Offers Today
Gap 01
No Healthy Carbonated Alternative
India's ₹30,000 Cr carbonated soft drink market delivers 25–35g sugar per serving. India has 101 million diabetics (IDF 2023). No Indian brand offers a functional, low-sugar carbonated RTD at accessible price.
Gap 02
Cognitive Fatigue Without Affordable Solutions
40%+ of Indian students report chronic cognitive fatigue and stress. No affordable, daily-use functional solution exists that combines GABA production with medicinal mushroom neuro-support. Source: NFHS-5.
Gap 03
Imported Supplements: ₹500–2,000/month
Foreign mushroom supplement brands (e.g. Four Sigmatic) cost ₹500–2,000/month — out of reach for students and young professionals. No Indian grain base, no fermentation, no cold-chain-free format.
