No time for real nutrition

The average working professional has 22 minutes for lunch, often at their desk. Students skip meals before exams. Athletes eat whatever is convenient after training. The food system was not designed for how Indians actually live.

Existing products deceive you

Most Indian health drinks are primarily sugar with token doses of vitamins your body cannot absorb. Cheap synthetic forms, under-dosed actives, misleading labels. The same brand sells better formulas in foreign markets. You deserve to know this.

Imported solutions do not fit India

Products designed for Western nutritional deficiencies miss what Indian bodies need. They do not understand Ragi, Moringa, or Ashwagandha. They do not understand how Indians eat, work, or train. India needs an Indian solution.

Solutions

We show our work.

Every formulation decision has a peer-reviewed reason. Every ingredient has a verified source. Every claim is something we can prove.

1

Active forms only

Most products use the cheapest permitted form of each vitamin and mineral. We use: Methylcobalamin, Pyridoxal-5-Phosphate, 5-MTHF Folate, Albion TRAACS® Chelated Minerals.

The difference: 3-4x better absorption. The cost difference per serving: ₹2. The choice was obvious.
2

Verified, not assumed

Our Lion's Mane extract is verified at minimum 30% β-glucan content using the Megazyme AOAC method — the gold standard verification method.

Not marketing weight. Not assumed potency. Verified active compound content. Most brands do not do this. We consider it non-negotiable.
3

Proving it, not just claiming it

We have designed a clinical study for 135 participants across 8 weeks — before we launch commercially. Primary outcomes: Biomarkers + cognitive tests.

Results will be published. Regardless of outcome. Ethics committee approval in progress.

Our Vision & Mission

Why Oxygen exists.

This is not a corporate origin story. This is the honest account of a problem we couldn't ignore, a gap nobody was filling, and a decision to build something better.

Building India's First Precision Nutrition System.

Sustainable Indigenous Circular
  • Sustainable Enterprise To build an innovation-driven biotechnology enterprise that prioritizes planetary health alongside human health.
  • Indigenous Resources To transform India's rich biological resources into globally competitive biocosmetic and food products.
  • Circular Bioeconomy To operate strictly through advanced biotechnology, circular bioeconomy principles, and responsible manufacturing.

To make world-class nutrition accessible to every ambitious Indian.

By combining India's ancient ingredient wisdom with modern nutritional science — and refusing to compromise on either.

Oxygen exists to close the gap between what urban Indians need nutritionally and what the market currently offers them.

"We believe Indian ingredients are world-class. We believe Indian consumers deserve products formulated to global scientific standards. We believe honesty about what is in a product is not a marketing strategy — it is a minimum standard that the industry has failed to meet."
  • 1. Product & Commercial Mission To develop and commercialize safe, natural, and sustainable biocosmetic and food products using innovative bioprocessing, fermentation, and formulation technologies, ensuring financial viability and market competitiveness.
  • 2. Research & Innovation Mission To strengthen indigenous biotechnology innovation by advancing research in bioprocess engineering, microbial fermentation, and nanotechnology, supported through government grants, academic collaboration, and applied research programs.
  • 3. Impact & Ecosystem Mission To create long-term environmental, social, and economic impact by supporting circular bioeconomy practices, enabling skill development through student mentorship and internships, generating employment, and aligning operations with national initiatives such as Make in India and sustainability-driven growth.

The People Behind Oxygen

Built by scientists.

We aren't nutritionists who became entrepreneurs. We are people who were frustrated by what existed and decided to fix it properly.

01 & 02 — The Origin

Founder

Chief Science Officer

A pharmaceutical scientist who turned fermentation into food — with the precision of a lab and the soul of a craft.

We spent six months trying to find a daily nutrition product we would actually recommend to someone we cared about. We could not find one. Either the science was inadequate, or the ingredients were compromised, or the price was inaccessible, or it was designed for a Western diet and simply repacked for India.

We analyzed blood reports from urban Indians across multiple cities. The pattern was consistent and alarming. Seven out of ten showed at least one significant deficiency. Most were health-conscious people. Most were not eating badly by any measure. Most had no idea they were deficient.

The food system was failing people who were trying to do the right thing. We decided the only option was to build it ourselves.

"I am building Oxygen because I am genuinely angry at what the market currently offers people who are trying to take their health seriously. I have seen the research. I know what good nutrition science looks like. And I know that the gap between what is possible and what is being sold is not technical — it is a choice. We are choosing differently."

Operating at intersection of: 🔬 Pharmaceutical Bioprocessing 🧬 Nanoscience 🌾 Fermented Food

03 — Our Approach
01

Science Before Marketing

We designed the formulation before we designed the brand. We chose ingredients before we chose colors. We cited our evidence before we wrote our copy. This is backwards from how most nutrition companies work. We think it is the only sensible order.

02

India Is Not a Market Segment

We did not take a Western formula and add Ashwagandha to make it Indian. We started from India. From what Indian bodies are deficient in. From what Indian lifestyles demand. India is not an afterthought — it is the foundation.

03

Transparency Is Not Optional

We will publish our lab reports. We will name our ingredient suppliers. We will cite the studies behind our claims. We will tell you when a study is preliminary and when it is robust. This should be standard.

04

Dose Matters As Much As Ingredient

Ashwagandha at 50mg is not the same as Ashwagandha at 300mg. Lion's Mane at 100mg is not what the research studied. We formulate at clinically relevant doses — not at doses that allow us to put the ingredient on the label.

05

Bioavailability Is the Real Metric

A 100mg dose with 5% absorption delivers 5mg to your body. A 50mg dose with 35% absorption delivers 17.5mg. The nutrient that reaches your bloodstream is the only nutrient that matters. We optimize for absorption first. Always.

06

Clinical Evidence Is Non-Negotiable

We designed our clinical study before we manufactured our first batch. Not because we are required to. But because we believe no company should sell a health product without evidence that it works.

04 — The Journey So Far

What we have built before we built anything physical. Most companies build first and think about science second. We spent months thinking about the science before building anything.

6+ months of nutritional deficiency research Completed

Analyzed ICMR, NFHS-5, WHO India data. Mapped exact deficiency profiles by demographic and lifestyle. Built the evidence base for product necessity.

Complete formulation design Completed

Reviewed 200+ peer-reviewed studies. Designed complete formulas for 3 drinks + 1 bar. Every ingredient evidence-based. Every dose clinically relevant.

Regulatory pathway fully mapped In Progress

FSSAI compliance audited per ingredient. Clinical study protocol designed. Ethics committee application in process. Label compliance framework completed.

TBI Incubation secured Active

Accepted by Technology Business Incubator at ACE, Hosur. Prototype development currently underway. Sensory testing beginning soon.
