73%

Vitamin D Deficiency

Urban India is running on empty.

Despite living under abundant sunlight, 73% of urban Indians are severely Vitamin D deficient. It cascades into immunity collapse, mood disorders, chronic fatigue, and bone disease. Most health drinks in India contain Vitamin D2 — the plant form your body barely converts. RIZE uses D3 cholecalciferol — the form that actually raises blood levels.

8%

Inorganic Iron Absorption Rate

You swallow it. Your body rejects it.

Ferrous sulfate — the standard iron in Horlicks, Complan, Bournvita — is absorbed at 8%. Iron bisglycinate (our form) absorbs at 58%. The cost difference per serving? About ₹2. Yet every major brand chooses the cheaper, worse form because it costs less and consumers don't know to ask.

40%

Indians carry MTHFR gene variant

Synthetic folic acid doesn't work for 40% of Indians.

The MTHFR gene variant prevents the body from converting synthetic folic acid into methylfolate — the biologically active form. If you're one of the 40%, your prenatal supplement, your B-complex, and your health drink's folic acid might be doing close to nothing. RIZE uses methylfolate — the form that works regardless of genetics.

Millet Base

India's forgotten supergrains — restored to their rightful place

Ragi (Finger Millet): 3.5× more calcium than milk. Lower glycemic index than rice or wheat. High in iron, fiber, and essential amino acids. Feeds millions of South Indians for centuries before we abandoned it.

Bajra (Pearl Millet): Rich in zinc, magnesium, phosphorus. Excellent source of B-vitamins naturally. Drought-resistant crop — sustainable farming at its core.

Jowar (Sorghum): High in antioxidants (3-deoxy anthocyanidins). Naturally gluten-free. Excellent binding matrix for bioactive compounds in formulation.

Mushroom Complex

Hot water extracts. β-glucan standardized. Clinically meaningful doses.

Lion's Mane (500mg, 30% β-glucan): Contains hericenones and erinacines — the only known food compounds that stimulate Nerve Growth Factor (NGF) synthesis. Studied for cognitive support, memory, and neurogenesis.

Cordyceps (200mg, 25% polysaccharides): Increases ATP production and cellular oxygen utilization. Used in sports nutrition for endurance. Shown to improve VO2 max in clinical studies.

Precision Micronutrients

Chelated minerals. Methylated vitamins. The forms that actually work.

Iron Bisglycinate (18mg): Absorbed at 58% vs 8% for ferrous sulfate. The chelate form substantially reduces GI side effects while maximizing absorption. Especially critical for Indian women aged 15–45.

Methylcobalamin B12 (2.5mcg): The active, neurally-available form of B12. Cyanocobalamin (used everywhere else) requires enzymatic conversion that fails in many individuals. For RIZE, it's methylcobalamin only.

5-MTHF Methylfolate (400mcg): MTHFR-safe. Works directly without the MTHFR enzyme step. Critical for pregnancy, cardiovascular health, and DNA methylation pathways.

The Bioavailability Argument

Your supplement is mostly marketing.

The difference between a supplement that works and one that doesn't isn't the ingredient name on the label. It's the chemical form — and whether your body can actually use it.

Every mainstream Indian health drink uses the cheapest ingredient forms available: ferrous sulfate (iron), cyanocobalamin (B12), folic acid (folate), zinc oxide, and Vitamin D2. These cost less and look identical on the nutrition label. The cost difference to the manufacturer per serving? About ₹2–4. The absorption difference to you? 5× to 7× less effective.
