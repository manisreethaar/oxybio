Why this matters

The gap is in delivery, not awareness.

57.0%

of women aged 15 to 49 in India are anaemic.

Source: NFHS-5 (2019–21), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.

Decades of supplementation have not closed India's micronutrient gaps. We are investigating whether a food people already drink every day can carry nutrition better than another tablet.
