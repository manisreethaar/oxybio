Research Associate

Full-time, on-site. 0 to 1 year of experience. ₹18,000 to ₹25,000 per month.

Designs and runs laboratory experiments, analyses data, maintains research documentation and supports regulatory paperwork. Requires a B.Tech, B.Sc or M.Sc in Food Technology, Biotechnology, Microbiology, Biochemistry or a related discipline.

Open

Research internships

For undergraduate and postgraduate students in Biotechnology, Microbiology, Food Science, Biochemistry or related fields, from any Indian college or university. One to six months.

Paid internships are offered for students contributing to active research over three to six months; the stipend is discussed at selection. Shorter or academic-project internships are unpaid and include a completion certificate, a work experience letter and a recommendation letter.

We never charge a fee for an internship. Anyone asking you to pay is not authorised by us.

Cycle leave

One day a month. No questions.

Every woman on the team can take one day off each month for menstrual health. No certificate, no explanation, no awkward conversation: a short message is enough.

No one at the company may question, discourage or comment on this leave. It is a right, not a favour.

We are a science company and we follow the evidence: supporting menstrual health supports wellbeing, productivity and trust.

Working here

Leave, hours and pay.

Hours and location

Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 6 pm, with a one-hour lunch break. Laboratory work is on-site at our laboratory in Hosur. Flexible arrangements for some roles are discussed at the offer stage.

Pay

Salaries and stipends are paid by bank transfer on or before the fifth working day of the following month, with a monthly pay slip. Pay is competitive for an incubator-stage startup; it is not yet comparable to a large established company.

Leave policy
Casual leave8 days a year, pro-rated from your joining month. Half-day and one-hour short permission available.
Sick leave8 days a year. No certificate needed for one or two days.
Cycle leave1 day a month, as above.
Maternity leave26 weeks, fully paid. Job, seniority and salary protected.
Paternity leave5 days paid, on the birth or adoption of a child.
Public holidaysAll Tamil Nadu Government notified public holidays. The list is shared every January.
Loss of payIf leave is exhausted, monthly gross divided by working days in that month.
An honest note

Where we are as an employer.

We are an early-stage startup, and that carries more uncertainty than an established company. Earned leave is not in our policy today; we will add it as we grow and give 30 days' notice when we do.

What we can offer now: direct mentorship, real ownership of your work, and your name on the research you contribute to.

We have zero tolerance for harassment, comply with the POSH Act, and handle every complaint confidentially.

How to apply

Send a CV and a short note.

Email your CV with three to five sentences on why you want to work here specifically. Generic cover letters are not shortlisted, and neither are applications that show no awareness of what we do. For internships, include your college, degree, year of passing and the dates you are available.
