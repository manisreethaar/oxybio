Platform

One grain base. Two formats.

The problem we are working on, what we are building, and the questions we still have to answer.

The problem

Micronutrient gaps in India have not closed.

57.0%

of women aged 15 to 49 in India are anaemic, up from 53.1% five years earlier.

Source: National Family Health Survey, NFHS-5 (2019–21) and NFHS-4 (2015–16), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.

Supplementation and fortification programmes have run for decades, yet the figure has moved in the wrong direction. Getting a nutrient onto a label is not the same as getting it into a person: a format has to be something people will consume every day, and the nutrition it carries has to be absorbed.

We think part of the answer is food rather than tablets, and food that fits habits people already have.

What we are building

From grain to format.

The base is the core of the work. The formats are how it reaches people. Both formats are in development and neither has a product name yet.

  1. Indigenous Indian grains

    Grains that have always been part of the Indian diet, grown in India.

  2. Fermented grain base

    Developed in our laboratory at TBI-DETI@ACE.

  3. Two formats

    Both built on the same base, both in development.

Where we fit

What we design for.

A daily habit

A format people reach for every day, not a regimen they have to remember.

An accessible price

Priced for everyday use in India, not as a premium import.

Indian origin

Built on Indian grains and Indian food culture, developed in Hosur.

Research questions

What we are investigating.

These are open questions, not product claims. Answers will be reported on the updates page as they are measured.

In progress

Composition

What the base contains, characterised by instrumental analysis, and how consistent that is from batch to batch.

Planned

Mineral bioavailability

Whether nutrients from the finished formats are absorbed, assessed through a properly designed study once the base is stable.

Planned

Sensory acceptance

Whether each format works on taste and texture, judged by people who are not on our team.

Planned

Safety and shelf life

Microbiological safety and shelf life, tested by an accredited third-party laboratory.

Our standard

Before anything is sold.

  • Accredited third-party testing of every format.
  • An FSSAI licence granted. The application is to be filed.
  • Every batch recorded end to end in OxyOS, our in-house digital quality system, from raw material to finished product.
