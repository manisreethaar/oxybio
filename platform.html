Platform

One grain base. Everyday formats.

We are developing a fermented powder made from indigenous Indian grains, designed to be reconstituted in carriers people already use every day: milk, and carbonated water. The work is at laboratory stage at TBI-DETI@ACE, Hosur.

The problem

Micronutrient gaps in India have not closed.

57.0%

of women aged 15 to 49 in India are anaemic, up from 53.1% five years earlier.

Source: National Family Health Survey, NFHS-5 (2019–21) and NFHS-4 (2015–16), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.

Supplementation and fortification programmes have run for decades, yet the figure has moved in the wrong direction. Getting a nutrient onto a label is not the same as getting it into a person: a format has to be something people will consume every day, and the nutrition it carries has to be absorbed.

We think part of the answer is food rather than tablets, and food that fits habits people already have.

What we are building

A base, and the formats it goes into.

The base is the core of the work. Formats are how it reaches a person. All names below are working descriptions, not product names.

Core

Grain base

A fermented powder made from indigenous Indian grains, developed in our laboratory at TBI-DETI@ACE. Shelf stability at ambient temperature is a design target under evaluation.

Format in development

Milk format

The base reconstituted in milk, for a familiar daily drink.

Format in development

Carbonated format

The base reconstituted in carbonated water, for a sparkling drink.

Research questions

What we are investigating.

These are open questions. None of them is a product claim, and we will report the answers as they are measured.

In progress

Composition

What the base contains, characterised by instrumental analysis, and how consistent that is from batch to batch.

Planned

Mineral bioavailability

Whether nutrients from the finished formats are absorbed, assessed through a properly designed study once the base is stable.

Planned

Sensory acceptance

Whether the base performs in each carrier on taste, texture and how well it disperses, judged by people who are not on our team.

Planned

Safety and stability

Microbiological safety and shelf life, tested by an accredited third-party laboratory before any product is offered for sale.

Our standard

What we hold ourselves to.

  • No claim is published before it has been measured.
  • Accredited third-party testing before any product is sold.
  • Results are reported whether they support our hypothesis or not.
  • Indigenous Indian grains as the foundation of the base.
  • No product is offered for sale until an FSSAI licence is granted. The application is to be filed.
